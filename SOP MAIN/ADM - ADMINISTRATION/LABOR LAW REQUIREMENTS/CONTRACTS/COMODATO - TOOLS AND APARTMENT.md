## ⚠️ Critical Leaks Identified (Read Before the Contract)

**LEAK 1 — The Fondo is your biggest vulnerability (still).**
Even reframed as "voluntary," a **daily $50 deduction via payroll** tied to housing is the single fact a judge will seize to reclassify Comodato → Arrendamiento. The fix is not just naming it differently. The contribution must be **legally severable from the housing itself**: the room must remain free *even if the worker contributes zero*. If contribution is a condition of staying, it is rent. The hardened clause makes non-contribution explicitly *non-grounds* for eviction, and routes the fund through a worker-controlled account the company never touches.

**LEAK 2 — "Forfeit of projected raises" is a disguised salary reduction.**
Art. 5 & 57 LFT: wages cannot be reduced, and any scheme that lowers effective pay can be voided. Framing it as forfeiting a "projected raise" is fragile because a raise that was promised can be argued as an acquired right. Safer: the contribution comes only from **already-received net salary as a voluntary authorized transfer** the worker can revoke at will (Art. 110 requires worker consent and revocability).

**LEAK 3 — The 24-hour vacate + abandoned-property donation risks *despojo* (criminal dispossession).**
If you pack and remove belongings without the worker's signed handover, you expose yourself to a *despojo* complaint — ironically handing them the criminal leverage. The fix: require a **witnessed inventory (acta) with two witnesses** at the moment of packing, photograph everything, and never dispose/donate until the 30-day window closes *with documented notice sent*.

**LEAK 4 — Off-grid resource "write-ups" need a defined standard.**
"Unjustified waste" is subjective and a judge will strike vague disciplinary triggers. It must reference an objective standard (the RIT Tabulador) and be tied to progressive discipline, not standalone punishment.

**LEAK 5 — Missing declarations weaken enforceability.**
The contract lacks: worker's civil capacity declaration, express citation of **Art. 2497 & 2500 Código Civil de Nayarit** (defining Comodato as gratuitous and terminable), acknowledgment that no *arrendamiento* is created, jurisdiction clause, and a **separability clause** so one voided clause doesn't collapse the whole instrument.
