**SECTION 1: COMPLETE PROPERTY TERRAIN & HAZARD REGISTRY**

_This section logs every potential physical variable present on the property across all seasons._

- **Hazard 1: The "Empedrado" (Cobblestone System)**
    - **Description:** Traditional rural Mexican cobblestone road bed consisting of heavy river stones embedded in dirt or weak concrete. Over time, heavy vehicles dislodge stones, leaving deep tire-catching gaps and protruding sharp edges.
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** High-frequency, destructive harmonic vibration. It looses fasteners, shears brackets, and rapidly cavitates (foams) shock absorber hydraulic fluid.
- **Hazard 2: "Terracería" (Dirt & Loose Rock Sections)**
    - **Description:** Unpaved sections consisting of native topsoil mixed with fractured volcanic rock and loose shale characteristic of the Nayarit coastline.
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** High risk of tire sidewall punctures, tread chunking, and flying rock debris thrown from tires into body panels or trailing riders.
- **Hazard 3: Steep Foothill Inclines (Sierra Madre Gradients)**
    - **Description:** Steep, continuous uphill climbs exceeding a 15% grade, built over the natural contours of the coastal hills.
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** Forces engines to operate under sustained maximum torque load at low speeds, resulting in rapid heat buildup, transmission stress, and clutch wear.
- **Hazard 4: Wet Red Clay Mud (Seasonal/Weather Dependent)**
    - **Description:** High-iron native soil that transforms into a slick, heavy, sticky paste when exposed to tropical rains (June–October).
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** Loss of traction leading to wheel spin, and mud packing inside wheel wells and chain guards, adding immense unsprung weight.
- **Hazard 5: Creek Water Crossings (Hydrological Hazards)**
    - **Description:** Active seasonal runoff streams that intersect the road bed, carrying silt, sand, and submerged moving rocks.
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** Thermal shock to hot engine/exhaust components, grease wash-out from bearings, and potential water ingestion into the engine intake if deep.
- **Hazard 6: Airborne "Cementante" & Red Clay Dust (Seasonal/Weather Dependent)**
    - **Description:** Micro-fine limestone-base road powder (cementante) and dried clay dust that becomes airborne during the dry season (November–May).
    - **Primary Vehicle Impact:** Internal engine ingestion, rapid air filter blockage, and abrasive scoring of moving seals.

**SECTION 2: CORPORATE COVERAGE MATRIX (UNAVOIDABLE INFRASTRUCTURE)**

_This section defines the specific elements the company accepts wear-and-tear liability for. These are the structural components of the road that **every driver must traverse**, regardless of their skill level, weather conditions, or driving choices._

The company covers accelerated wear and damage caused **exclusively** by the following infrastructure items over the 4km daily route:

**A. Covered Infrastructure Variables**

1. **The Fixed Cobblestone (Empedrado) Network:** Any damage resulting from the non-stop mechanical vibration of the stones.
2. **The Compacted Dirt/Rock Road (Terracería):** Any damage resulting from regular contact with the baseline dry rock surface.
3. **The Established Incline (Uphill/Downhill Grades):** Any strain or wear resulting purely from the gravity and torque required to climb the property's hills.

**B. Eligible Motorcycle Components Under Corporate Coverage**

If an employee drives a personal motorcycle on the property, the company provides maintenance allowances or repair coverage _only_ for the following components damaged by the infrastructure listed above:

- **Suspension Systems (100% Covered):** Blown front fork seals, scored fork tubes, and rear shock absorber failure caused by the cobblestone vibrations.
- **Wheel Assemblies & Tires (100% Covered):** Bent or cracked rims, loosened spokes requiring wheel truing, tire tread chunking from terracería rocks, and pinch-flat inner tubes caused by impacting fixed stones.
- **Clutch Packs & Friction Plates (100% Covered):** Premature glazing or burning of clutch plates caused strictly by the engine loading required to scale the steep uphill gradients.
- **Chassis & Structural Mounts (100% Covered):** Sheared exhaust hangers, cracked exhaust pipes at the manifold, loosened engine mount bolts, and frame weld fatigue failures caused by structural vibrations.
- **Wheel Bearings (100% Covered):** Pitted ball bearings or distorted bearing races caused by the heavy physical pounding of the uneven road bed.

**C. Explicit Driver-Mitigated Exclusions (Not Covered by the Company)**

Per your operating guidelines, the following damages are classified as **avoidable hazards** resulting from driver choices, improper operation, or poor timing, and are **excluded** from standard company wear-and-tear coverage:

- Water damage, engine hydro-locking, or washed-out grease from choosing to cross deep creek water instead of waiting for levels to drop or using designated bypasses.
- Clutch burning or structural damage sustained from getting stuck while attempting to drive through wet, active red clay mud during or immediately after heavy rain events.
- Cosmetic or electrical damage caused by driving closely behind other vehicles in dense dust clouds.
