## I. Identity & Fiscal Shield (Onboarding)

This phase locks in their legal identity. If an employee tries to sue you using a different legal name or claims the resort is their only domicile, this paperwork shuts them down instantly. This documentation serves as a proof of person and existence within the system and a backup DOxX to have a full readout on their personal life

| Document                                            | Execution Details                                                                         | Strategic Purpose                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Identificación Oficial (INE/Pasaporte)**          | Scanned copy signed by the employee on the margin.                                        | Proves legal identity and prevents identity fraud in labor tribunals.            |
| **Comprobante de Domicilio**                        | Recent utility bill from a location outside the resort.                                   | Establishes a civil domicile off-property to defeat squatter claims.             |
| **Constancia de Situación Fiscal**                  | Official SAT document required before the first shift.                                    | Mandatory to legally stamp payroll (CFDI) and avoid tax discrepancies.           |
| **Documento NSS y CURP**                            | Downloaded from official government portals.                                              | Required for IMSS registration to avoid catastrophic medical liability.          |
| **Carta de Designación de Beneficiarios**           | Employee writes the name of their legal heir in blue ink.                                 | Prevents multiple family members from suing the company for death benefits.      |
| **Solicitud de Empleo Firmada**                     | Standard physical application with their stated background.                               | Locks in their stated experience to justify termination if they lied.            |
| Documentaction adicional requerida por el empleador | Social media handles, any and all rpfiles where they regularly conduct an online presence | This is to verify compliance with NDA and document any breach in their contract] |
