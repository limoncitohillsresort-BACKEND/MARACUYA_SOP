## Cláusulas de Operación del Tabulador

**Artículo 27.- Ventana de Reincidencia.**
La reincidencia se computa dentro de un periodo rodante de **90 días naturales**. Tres (3) amonestaciones escritas (S2 o superior) por faltas de igual o distinta naturaleza dentro de dicho periodo constituirán una conducta habitual, actualizando la causal de rescisión del Artículo 47-XV.

**Artículo 28.- Límite Legal de Suspensión.**
Ninguna suspensión excederá **8 días** conforme al Artículo 423, Fracción X de la LFT. La suspensión se ejecuta previa acta administrativa con dos testigos.

**Artículo 29.- Derecho de Audiencia.**
Antes de imponer cualquier sanción S3 o superior, se otorgará al trabajador el derecho de manifestar lo que a su interés convenga, asentándose su versión en la propia acta. La negativa a firmar NO invalida el acta si es firmada por dos testigos.

**Artículo 30.- Gravedad Circunstancial.**
El patrón se reserva el derecho de elevar el nivel de sanción cuando la falta, aunque catalogada como leve, ocurra frente a huéspedes VIP, comprometa la seguridad, o afecte la reputación del resort.

The 90-day reincidence window (Art. 27) is what legally converts "minor annoyances" into a defensible Art. 47-XV firing. Without a written accumulation rule, a judge sees isolated events; with it, the judge sees a documented pattern of *conducta habitual*.

Giving the employee a chance to explain — and documenting it — kills the future claim that discipline was "arbitrary" or "retaliatory." It costs you nothing and neutralizes their strongest narrative.

## Strategic Notes for Deployment

**1. The Blank-Signature Proof Is Your Whole Defense**
The single most powerful piece of evidence you can hold is a stack of comodatos where the main contract is signed but **Section Four is deliberately left blank on several of them.** Encourage genuine opt-outs. Do not pressure universal participation. The moment 100% of workers "voluntarily" sign identical amounts, the voluntariness becomes fiction and a judge sees through it.

**2. Physically Separate the Signature Page**
Print Section Four on its **own sheet** so it can be signed, left blank, or added later without touching the main comodato. This reinforces that it is a distinct legal act, not baked into the housing agreement.

**3. Keep the Facilitator Wall Intact (Art. 4.4)**
The instant LA EMPRESA touches the money — holds it, banks it, accounts for it — the "facilitator only" defense collapses and it looks like the company is collecting rent. The third-party/committee account must be **real and demonstrably independent**. Keep bank records proving the company never controls it.

**4. Match Paper to Practice**
Same warning as before, now more acute: the legal text is airtight, but enforceability depends on the *lived reality* matching it. Document real variation — different amounts, some zeros, actual revocations honored without retaliation. That behavioral record is what makes the clause survive scrutiny.

**5. Interaction with the Cross-Default Clause**
Confirm that non-participation in the Fondo is **explicitly excluded** from any *falta* or cross-default trigger. A worker declining to donate can never be disciplined or have their comodato dissolved for that reason. Your Cláusula Décima (cross-default) should never reach Section Four.
